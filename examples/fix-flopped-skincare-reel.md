## The re-cut: "I just washed my face"

**Idea in one sentence:** One wash can leave the day's sunscreen and makeup on your skin, and an oil cleanser first is what gets it off.
**Goal:** sales (the $19 routine guide)
**Format:** Fact with a twist, filmed with busy hands
**Length:** 35 s
**Chain:** Stop: curiosity (the obvious answer is wrong) -> Hold: open loop -> Pay off: satisfying reveal, and the fix is step one of the guide ("doubt, test, proof")

### Hook (0-3 s)

- **See:** a tight shot at the sink, hair clipped back, one half of your face freshly washed and still damp. A white cotton pad is already sweeping down that cheek. By second one it's turned to the lens, and it's beige. Start the clip mid-swipe.
- **Read:** I just washed my face
- **Hear:** "I just washed my face. So what's this?"
- **Question it opens:** "If washing didn't get that off, what's still on my face, and what does get it off?"

**Alternates to test**

1. **Arm stripes.** Your forearm under the tap with three stripes on it (sunscreen, foundation, mascara). You lather face wash over them, rinse, and they're still there. Text: "Face wash. Still there." First line: "I've just washed this." The oil cleanser melting the stripes becomes the reveal. Use this one if your pad doesn't show much or you wear untinted SPF.
2. **Start on the result.** Two pads already stuck to the mirror, one beige, one white. Text: "Same face. Same face wash." First line: "Both of these came off my face tonight. One thing was different." Then rewind to show how. Same footage, re-edited.
3. **Start on the melt.** Close-up of oil cleanser being massaged into the unwashed cheek, foundation dissolving into it. Text: "This goes on before face wash". First line: "This goes on before my face wash. Here's what happens when I skip it." Then the dirty pad. Same footage, re-edited.

### Payoff and call to action

- **Payoff:** the second pad answers the opening question. The same face wash leaves a clean pad once oil has gone on first. The small turn is that the face wash was never the problem, which changes how the opening reads.
- **Call to action:** link in bio for the routine guide. "Follow for more" is cut.

### Caption and cover

- **Caption:** Try it after your normal wash tonight and see what colour your pad comes back. This is step one. The whole routine, in order, is in my guide (link in bio).
- **Hashtags:** #doublecleansing #oilcleanser #skincareroutine #skincaretips
- **Cover text:** Washed. Still not clean.

### Shoot and edit notes

- **You need:** phone propped at face height beside the mirror, your usual bathroom light, a hair clip, four or five white cotton pads, micellar water or toner to dampen them (the same amount on each), your oil cleanser, your usual face wash and a towel. Wear your normal tinted SPF or foundation that day.
- **Order on the day:** wash one half of your face with face wash only, exactly as you normally do and for as long as you normally do. Then start filming. The other half stays unwashed until the 12-second mark.
- **The one shot that must be right:** the first pad turned to the lens. The residue has to be obvious on a phone screen within the first second, so bring the pad close to the camera, and hold a clean pad next to it if the contrast is weak.
- **Slow down:** from 23 s. Sweep the second pad slowly, pause before turning it, then leave both pads still for a full second.
- **Cut:** the greeting, the agenda, moisturizer, SPF, "that's it" and "follow for more".
- **Leave in:** if the second pad isn't spotless, show it anyway and say so. A faint trace next to the first pad still makes the point, and it reads as honest.
- **Voice:** talk live while your hands work, and add captions.

### Check

- **Passes:**
  - Stop: the first frame is the test itself, there is no greeting, the hook text is five words, and it works muted.
  - Hold: one idea, the dirty pad stays in shot as the open question, the explanation comes after the curiosity, hands move throughout, and it slows before the reveal.
  - Pay off: the ending answers the opening and there is one call to action. The weakest pass is the guide, which follows the reveal as the next step instead of being the reveal. Showing the page on your phone is what keeps it from feeling tacked on.
  - Craft: you can film it alone at your sink, and a viewer can follow it without the words.
  - Honesty: it's a real test, and it claims nothing about what leftover product does to skin, only what the pad shows.
- **Risks left:**
  - Everything depends on the first pad showing visible residue. Run the test off camera first. If your pad comes back nearly clean, don't under-wash to force it; shoot alternate 1 instead.
  - The ending confirms what viewers expect. It's a reveal, not a twist, so the pause and the "same face wash" line have to carry it. If the new retention graph sags near the end, this is why.
  - No plan guarantees a view count. This one removes the avoidable reasons the last reel stalled.
- **Test first:** alternate 2. It uses the same footage, so it costs a re-edit, not a re-shoot.
