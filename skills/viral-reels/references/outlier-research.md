# Finding outliers worth modelling

An outlier is a video that travelled far beyond its creator's normal reach. It is the best evidence available that a format works, because the creator's audience size cannot explain the result. Study outliers, not big accounts: a celebrity's average video proves nothing about structure.

## The method (a few minutes per search)

1. **Search the niche.** Type a niche keyword into the platform's search and open the short-video results. Try three or four keywords, including the audience's own words for their problem.
2. **Spot candidates.** Look for view counts that stand out in the results.
3. **Vet each one on the creator's profile.** Compare the video's views with:
   - the creator's follower count, and
   - the views on their other recent videos.

   As a rule of thumb, treat it as an outlier when views are several times the follower count, or roughly ten times what the creator usually gets. A small account with one enormous video is the strongest signal of all.
4. **Check that the format repeats.** Has the same structure worked for other creators, or for this creator more than once? One hit can be luck; a format that travels across accounts is a pattern.
5. **Prefer recent.** What worked in the last few months says more about today's feed than a hit from years ago.
6. **Break it down.** Use Breakdown mode: the three-beat chain, multipliers, and the structure stated without the content.
7. **Adapt.** Recreate the pattern with the user's own subject, style and twist. Never the content.

## Mining the user's own account

The user's own outliers are even better evidence, because the audience is theirs.

1. List their videos by views and find the usual level (the middle of the list, not the average, so one hit does not distort it).
2. Take the top three that clearly beat that level.
3. Break each down. What do the three have in common in the first three seconds?
4. Make those again with new subjects before trying anything new.

## Keeping a log

Record each outlier so patterns show up over time:

| Link | Creator followers | Usual views | This video | Stop | Hold | Pay off | Format | What to take |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

When the same chain appears three times in the log, it is a format for this niche. Suggest adding any new trigger to `triggers.md`.

## If you (Claude) are doing the browsing

- Ask before using a signed-in browser session, and only read: do not like, follow, comment or message.
- Captions rarely explain a video. The content is in the footage and on-screen text, so sample frames through the video, or ask the user to describe the opening and the ending.
- View counts are visible on a profile's short-video tab. Use them for the vetting step.
- Report what you could not see. Do not fill gaps in a video you did not watch.
