# Pre-publish checklist

Run every plan through this before delivering it. Each line is a yes/no question; a "no" means fix the plan, not explain it away. Report the outcome in the plan's Check section: which groups pass, and the one or two real risks left.

## Stop

- Does the very first frame show something worth stopping for, before any word is read or heard?
- Is the stop trigger named, and is its mechanism visible on screen (not only described in the voiceover)?
- Does the opening avoid greetings, logos, intros and "in this video"?
- Is the on-screen hook text seven words or fewer and readable with the sound off?

## Hold

- Is a question open in the viewer's head by second three, and still open at the midpoint?
- Does curiosity come before answers? (If the opening gives the answer away, there is no reason to stay.)
- Is there exactly one idea?
- Does every row of the beat sheet have a job? Any second that could be cut without loss should be cut.
- Is something moving or changing on screen at least every few seconds?
- Is there a build toward the payoff, with a slight slow-down just before it?

## Pay off

- Does the ending answer the question the hook opened, or deliberately withhold it for a stated reason?
- Does the ending break the expectation the middle built?
- If there is a product, does it arrive as the answer rather than as an interruption?
- Is there one call to action, and does it match the goal (reach, comments, follows, sales)?
- Is there a reason to send this to a specific person, save it, or watch it twice?

## Craft

- Is the message shown rather than told wherever possible?
- Could a viewer who does not speak the language follow the main beat?
- Can the user actually film this with what they have?
- Is the length justified, or is it long because nothing was cut?

## Honesty and safety

- Is every claim and number true?
- Does the payoff honour the hook, so nobody feels baited?
- Is any staged moment resolved as staged, with no fake warnings or fake news?
- Is the opening shot safe to film?
- Is the structure borrowed and the content original?

## After publishing

- Did it beat the account's usual views by a clear margin? If yes, make it again with a new subject before trying something new.
- If not, where did viewers leave? Early means the stop beat, middle means the hold, late means a payoff they saw coming.
