# Case library

Worked breakdowns of real outlier videos, for precedent when choosing a chain. Each entry: what happens, the chain, and the lesson.

The cases are public short-form videos that far outperformed their creator's usual reach, studied in November 2026. View counts are approximate, as publicly visible at the time.

## Danger and tension openings

**Two cars (25M).** A parked car opens its door as another speeds toward it; the second car passes through impossibly.
Chain: danger -> prediction trap -> impossible twist.
Lesson: a visible threat buys the first seconds; the twist must beat the prediction.

**Night drive that becomes a house tour (4.5M, real estate).** Dashcam speeding at a crossing; the screen swipes into ordinary phone apps; it ends as a property tour and never returns to the "accident".
Chain: visual tension -> pattern interrupt -> unclosed loop.
Lesson: the unresolved thread filled the comments with people asking what happened.

**Seasonal candy warning (4.4M in a day, real estate).** Opens like a warning to check children's sweets; something is hidden inside; the reveal is a house listing.
Chain: fear -> "what's inside?" -> surprise, plus timing.
Lesson: tying the hook to the season made it instantly relevant. Note: fake safety warnings are over the line in this skill; keep the timing lesson, not the fake warning.

**Near-miss in a car (several ads, 6.7M to 13M).** Dashcam plus driver reaction to a staged near-crash; the product appears late.
Chain: urgency or "is this real?" -> prediction -> surprise with a delayed product reveal.
Lesson: holding the product to the end carries retention all the way to it.

**Product frozen in a block (21-22M, several creators).** An item sealed in ice or gel is about to be smashed.
Chain: object under threat -> slowed tension -> clean satisfying reveal.
Lesson: waiting makes the payoff land harder.

## Pattern interrupts

**Giant snowball (32M in two days, car ad).** Someone shoves a huge snowball toward an edge.
Chain: pattern interrupt -> "is he going to push it?" -> attention transfer.
Lesson: at the moment of payoff the video cuts straight to the product.

**Giant light switch (4.7M, knitting pattern).** An oversized switch on a real wall.
Chain: pattern interrupt -> open loop -> unexpected payoff.
Lesson: a satisfying reveal with no sales pitch still sold.

**Potter's work falling (300M).** A calm craft video suddenly shows the pots collapsing.
Chain: pattern interruption -> curiosity loop -> closure at the end.
Lesson: do not post the work straight; break an expectation and open a loop.

**Fake hair on the screen (creator with 5K followers).** A dessert video with what looks like a hair on the viewer's own screen.
Chain: pattern interrupt carried by one tiny detail.
Lesson: the account's other videos had low views, so one detail did the work.

## Broken reality

**Car leaving the post.** A video dressed as a normal post; the car drives or drifts out over the likes and caption. Two brands, one with a caption joke that matched the stunt.
Chain: familiarity -> frame break.
Lesson: it worked because it was different, not because it was better.

**Sauce over the caption.** A food post where the sauce spills out of the picture over the interface. No words.
Chain: broken reality alone.
Lesson: a split second of confusion is enough to stop the thumb; other creators copied the format successfully.

## People and situations

**Man eating alone (13.7M).** Filmed by a stranger with a mocking caption; then the recording turns cinematic and it is revealed as staged, the man completely unbothered.
Chain: witness -> pattern break -> flipped expectation.
Lesson: any emotion (sympathy, curiosity, judgment) stops the scroll; the flip is what gets shared.

**Coworkers doing a trend (42M).** Ordinary staff in an ordinary workplace; the catch goes differently than expected and the promotion lands inside the surprise.
Chain: familiarity -> prediction trap -> ad as punchline.
Lesson: do not interrupt the content with the ad.

**Awkward family scene (24M, blanket brand).** A cinematic relatable setup; the product changes the scene.
Chain: story -> unexpected twist -> emotional payoff.
Lesson: story, surprise, product. The ad became entertainment.

**"Abandoned" bundle.** A sad-looking discovery that turns out to be something harmless.
Chain: expectation -> emotional misdirection -> playful surprise.
Lesson: people replay to see how they were fooled and then share; replays plus shares drive reach.

**Announcement with an unreadable message (16M).** A creator "reveals" text that is glitched or too small while friends also try to read it.
Chain: curiosity gap -> shared waiting -> the announcement as payoff.
Lesson: do not just announce; make people curious enough to wait.

## Curiosity openings

**Falling from a plane (6.7M, two real estate agents).** A point-of-view fall with a counter-intuitive fact; the "better landing spot" is the house for sale.
Chain: question whose obvious answer is wrong -> explanation -> the reveal is the offer.
Lesson: two creators used the same structure and both went viral.

**Talking while the hands work (53M, many creators).** Speaking to camera while cutting fruit or similar.
Chain: movement hook -> dual attention.
Lesson: not too distracting, only enough to keep the eyes busy.

**Hook patterns.** Three fill-in-the-blank openings: a surprising result from an odd method; an identity that refuses the behaviour expected of it; a goal followed by an A-or-B choice.

## Craft and process

**Coffee pour that goes wrong.** The flawless pour got a fraction of the views of the failed one.
Chain: imperfection -> surprise -> connection.
Lesson: perfection is predictable; sometimes the mistake is the content.

**Behind the scenes of a shoot.** Shows the takes and crew, stops before the result, which is on the profile.
Chain: behind the scenes -> process -> missing payoff.
Lesson: views turned into follows because closure required a profile visit.

**Same trick shot, two cuts.** The flop had extra words and a rushed punchline; the hit used three one-word labels and slowed down before the end.
Lesson: cut the fat and build tension. Friction limits reach.

## Lessons about method

- **Outliers over averages.** Look for videos far above the creator's follower count; that shows the content travelled beyond the existing audience.
- **A repeated opening.** One creator tested dozens of openings, found one that worked and kept using it, always starting from a distance as if the viewer were about to witness something.
- **Repeat winners.** Accounts that returned to a subject that had already worked saw the second version perform too.
- **Structure, not content.** Recreate the pattern and add your own twist, style and perspective.
- **The nine rules** behind all of it: earn the stop in three seconds; curiosity before answers; show, don't tell; one idea; no dead seconds; build anticipation; break expectations; give a reason to share; study proven formats and adapt them.
