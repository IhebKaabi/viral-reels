There's this reel from a tiny bakery, like 3k followers, that got 2.1 million views. It starts with a tray of perfect croissants on the counter, then the baker's elbow knocks the whole tray toward the edge and the video freezes right before they fall with text 'guess how many survived'. Then it shows her counting the survivors one by one, and the last shot is her eating the one broken croissant with the caption 'quality control'. Why did this work? I run a dental clinic and want to do something like it.
